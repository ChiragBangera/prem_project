What is this tab? Understat has no touches, pressures or carry data — only shots. This tab pulls StatsBomb open data (a separate, free event-level source) for the metrics Understat cannot provide: touches (incl. opposition-box), pressures (with the 5-second successful rule), carry distance, ball recoveries, passing and final-third entries. Coverage is limited to its free competitions (La Liga 2004–2021, Champions League 2004–2019, World Cups) — no current Premier League — so nothing here mixes with the Understat views.
How to use:
  1. Pick a competition / season (e.g. La Liga 2020/21).
  2. Pick a match from that season.
  3. Analyze → team touch/press/carry profiles + per-player leaderboards.
